How it works

One line in, one verdict out.

Paste a link, share to LinkShield, or scan a QR. That's the whole interaction.

01 — SEND IT

Share or paste a link

From WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS, or any app — straight to LinkShield.

02 — WE CHECK

Checked against threat data

Google Safe Browsing, URLhaus, and domain heuristics — a rules engine, not a guess.

03 — YOU DECIDE

A verdict you understand

Green, yellow, or red — with a plain-language reason underneath.

The verdict system

Every link hits the databases first.

Checked live against Google Safe Browsing and URLhaus — confirmed phishing and malware lists.

Then we inspect the domain itself.

How old is it? Does it redirect through other sites? Does it typo-squat a brand like "gcash-verify.net"?

One score. No black box.

A deterministic rules engine combines every signal into a single 0–100 risk score, then stamps a verdict.

  • Safe Browsing
  • URLhaus
  • Domain age: 3 days
  • Redirect chain: 2 hops
  • Typosquat match: gcash
